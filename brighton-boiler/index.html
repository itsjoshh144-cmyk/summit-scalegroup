FAQ

Common questions

Do you cover my area?

We cover Brighton (BN1, BN2), Hove (BN3) and Portslade (BN41). Use the postcode checker above, or just ask: if you're nearby we'll tell you honestly whether we can help.

How much will it cost?

Every boiler and home is different, so we don't guess over the phone. We'll explain the problem and give you a clear price before any work starts, so there are no surprises.

How often should my boiler be serviced?

Once a year is the usual recommendation, and most manufacturers need an annual service to keep the warranty valid. Check your boiler's paperwork for the exact terms.

Which boilers do you work on?

All the major brands, including Worcester Bosch, Vaillant, Baxi, Ideal, Viessmann and Glow-worm. If you're not sure what you've got, a photo of the front of the boiler helps.

Are you Gas Safe registered?

Yes. Every engineer carries their Gas Safe ID card, and you're welcome to check it when they arrive.

What should I do if I smell gas?

Leave the property, don't use electrical switches or naked flames, and call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999. It's free and open 24/7. Once it's safe, we can carry out the repair.

Need your heating looked at?

Send a quick enquiry or give us a call. We'll get back to you during working hours.
