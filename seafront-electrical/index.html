Power cut, sparks or a burning smell?

For a power cut, call 105 (free) to reach your local network operator. If you see sparks or smoke or smell burning, switch off at the fuse board only if it's safe, keep clear, and call 999 if there's a fire. Once it's safe, we can find and fix the fault.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you cover my area?

We cover Brighton (BN1, BN2), Hove (BN3), Portslade (BN41), Shoreham (BN43), Lancing (BN15) and Worthing (BN11 to BN14). Use the postcode checker above, or just ask.

How much will it cost?

It depends on the job, so we don't guess over the phone. We'll look at the work, explain what's needed and give you a clear price before anything starts.

I'm a landlord. How often do I need an EICR?

In England, private landlords must have the electrical installation inspected and tested by a qualified person at least every five years, and give a copy of the report to tenants. Check the latest government guidance for the full rules.

Does my fuse board need replacing?

Not always. Many older boards can stay as they are. We'll inspect it, explain what we find and only recommend an upgrade if there's a real reason for one.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. You'll get the right certificate for the work we do, and notifiable work is registered so it complies with Building Regulations.

What should I do in a power cut?

Check whether neighbours have lost power too. If they have, call 105 (free) to reach your local network operator. If it's just your home, check your fuse board for a tripped switch, and call us if it won't reset.
