FAQ

Questions trades ask us.

How quickly can this be installed?

It depends on how much you want it to handle, and we'll give you a realistic timescale during your demo. There's no hardware. It's set up around your services and area, and you test it yourself before it goes anywhere near a customer.

Will customers know it's AI?

It answers in your business name and speaks naturally, no keypad menus or hold music. You decide how it introduces itself. Either way callers get a prompt, polite answer instead of a voicemail beep.

What happens if it can't answer a question?

It doesn't guess and it doesn't invent a price. It takes the details, flags it to you, and you tell us the answer so it's handled correctly next time.

Can it transfer urgent calls?

Urgent-call transfer and text alerts can be configured as part of your setup. You decide what counts as urgent — a leak, no heating, a lockout — and how those calls should be handled.

Can it work outside business hours?

Out-of-hours answering, including evenings and weekends, can be configured for your business. You decide which calls should be handled out of hours and how.

Can I customise what it says?

Every part of it. Services you offer, postcodes you cover, call-out charges, the questions it asks before booking. Send us a change and we'll update it.

How is this different from an answering service?

An answering service takes a message. This takes the job — it knows your trade and qualifies the enquiry instead of just writing down a name and number, and it doesn't put callers in a queue.

What happens to my customers' details?

They're used to get the enquiry to you. We don't sell or share them, and we'll delete them on request. We'll walk you through exactly how data is handled during setup.

What if I don't like it?

No minimum term. 30 days' notice and your calls go back to ringing your mobile exactly as before. Your number was never ours to hold onto.
