Questions trades ask us

How quickly can this be installed?

Setup takes as little as 48 hours, depending on how much detail you want it trained on. There's no hardware and no new handset — you keep your existing number and set a call divert, we train it on your services, coverage area and pricing, then you ring it yourself and test it before it goes anywhere near a customer.

Will customers know it's AI?

It answers in your business name and speaks naturally, with no keypad menus or hold music. You decide how it introduces itself — a straightforward greeting, or openly stating it's a virtual receptionist. Either way the caller gets a prompt, polite answer instead of a voicemail beep, and in our experience that's the bit they actually care about.

What happens if it can't answer a question?

It doesn't guess and it doesn't make up a price. It says it'll have the right person call back, takes the details and flags it to you. Then you tell us the answer and it never gets caught out on that one again.

Can it transfer urgent calls?

Yes. You set the rules for what counts as urgent — a leak, no heating, a lockout — and those calls go straight through to your mobile or the on-call engineer. If nobody picks up, it takes the full details and texts you within seconds so nothing is lost.

Can it work outside business hours?

It answers 24 hours a day, including evenings, weekends and bank holidays. You can run a different script out of hours, quote your emergency call-out rate up front, and decide which jobs are worth getting out of bed for.

Can I customise responses?

Every part of it: the services you offer and the ones you turn down, the postcodes you cover, call-out charges, the boiler brands you're accredited for, the questions asked before a booking, and the tone of voice. Send us a change and it's live the same day.

How is this different from an answering service?

An answering service takes a message. This takes the job. It knows your trade, your prices and your diary, it qualifies the enquiry rather than writing down a name and number, and it doesn't bill you by the minute or put callers in a queue at half seven on a Sunday.

What happens to my customers' details?

They're used for one thing: getting the enquiry to you. Call records and lead details are stored securely, never sold or shared with third parties, and deleted on request. We handle personal data in line with UK GDPR, and you can ask for a copy of everything we hold on your business at any time.

What if I don't like it?

There's no minimum term. Give us 30 days' notice and your calls go back to ringing your mobile exactly as they did before. You keep your number throughout — it was never ours to hold onto.
